Privacy Policy

Marian Kordney Mail Desk · Last updated: November 22, 2026

Who uses Marian Kordney Mail Desk

Marian Kordney Mail Desk is a private, self-hosted tool operated by an application-help service ("we"). It is used only by us, to manage the job-search mailboxes of people we assist ("mailbox owners"). A mailbox is connected only when its owner signs in with Google or Microsoft and approves access.

What data is accessed

With the mailbox owner's permission, Marian Kordney Mail Desk accesses:

  • Gmail (Google OAuth scope gmail.modify): email headers (sender, recipients, subject, date) and message content, and Gmail labels.
  • Outlook / Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Graph permissions Mail.ReadWrite, Mail.Send, User.Read, offline_access): the same email data, and Outlook categories.
  • The account's email address and display name, to show which mailbox is which.

Marian Kordney Mail Desk does not access contacts, calendars, files or account settings, and never deletes emails permanently.

How the data is used

  • To sort emails into categories (for example: Applied, Interview, Rejected) and tag them in the mailbox.
  • To show emails in a private dashboard so we can read them and reply, forward or save drafts on the owner's behalf.
  • To send a short private notification (sender and subject) to our own Slack when an email needs action.

The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

Third parties

To classify an email and to draft replies, a shortened excerpt (sender, subject and the start of the text, with phone numbers removed) is sent to the DeepSeek API. Verification codes and security alerts are not sent there. Notifications to Slack contain only the sender, subject and a short preview. No other third party receives email data.

Storage and security

Data is stored on our own server. Access tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM), and the dashboard is protected by a password. Passwords of the email accounts are never seen or stored: sign-in happens on Google's or Microsoft's own pages.

Retention and removal

Disconnecting a mailbox in Marian Kordney Mail Desk deletes that mailbox's stored data. Mailbox owners can also remove access at any time: Google accounts at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Microsoft accounts at microsoft.com/consent.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Marian Kordney Mail Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
