Gmail-Inbox-2307

An email organizer for job applications.

Gmail-Inbox-2307 is a private, self-hosted tool used by an application-help service to manage the mailboxes of people it assists during their job search. It works with Gmail and Outlook accounts. It reads new emails, sorts them into categories such as Applied, Interview and Rejected, tags them in the mailbox, and shows emails that need a reply in a single dashboard.

Gmail-Inbox-2307 is not a public service and no one can sign up. A mailbox is connected only when its owner signs in with Google or Microsoft and approves access, and the owner can remove that access at any time.
